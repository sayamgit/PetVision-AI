On your phone, even when PetVision is closed

Let your calendar remind you.

  1. Save a reminder here.
  2. Choose Add to calendar below and open the downloaded .ics file in your calendar.
  3. Check the date, time and notification setting, then save the event.

The file requests a notification 30 minutes before. Your calendar and device settings control delivery; check that alerts are enabled.

ⓘ Google Calendar & privacy

For Google Calendar on a computer: Settings → Import & export → Import the .ics file. Open the imported event and check or add a notification. On Apple devices, open the file with Calendar and review its alert.

The file contains your pet's name and reminder title, so choose a private calendar. It excludes private notes, medical details, contacts and photos.

Calendar entries are separate copies. Completing or deleting a reminder here does not update your calendar. Avoid importing the same reminder twice. PetVision does not send email or push notifications.
